\begin{weeklist}

\item[Week 4] Electronic Markets \& the Limit Order Book: how electronic
  markets function, order types, exchange structures, and the mechanics
  of the limit order book. \textit{(Cartea Ch.~1)}

\item[Week 5] Market Microstructure: market making models, trading costs,
  liquidity measurement, adverse selection, and price formation.
  \textit{(Cartea Ch.~2)}

\item[Week 6] Stochastic Optimal Control: the mathematical framework
  underlying algorithmic execution strategies: dynamic programming,
  Hamilton, Jacobi Bellman equations, and optimal stopping.
  \textit{(Cartea Ch.~5)}

\item[Week 7] Market Making: inventory-based and adverse-selection
  market-making models; how market makers choose quotes and manage risk.
  \textit{(Cartea Ch.~10)}

\end{weeklist}
